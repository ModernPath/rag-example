# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy applies to all business travel at Polar Pixel Oy (fictional sample content).

## Booking travel

All flights, trains, and hotels are booked through TravelDesk, our travel agency.
Book at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Trips must be approved
by your manager before booking.

## Flights

Economy class is the default. Business class is allowed for flights longer than
six hours, or when travelling overnight directly to a customer meeting.

Prefer trains for domestic trips under four hours, such as Tampere–Helsinki.

## Hotels

The maximum hotel price per night, including taxes, is:

- Helsinki: 160 EUR
- Stockholm: 190 EUR
- Berlin: 170 EUR
- London: 230 EUR
- Other cities: 150 EUR

If a conference hotel is more expensive, ask your manager for an exception before
booking.

## Daily allowance

Polar Pixel pays the Finnish Tax Administration daily allowance (per diem):

- Full domestic daily allowance (over 10 hours): 51 EUR
- Partial domestic daily allowance (over 6 hours): 24 EUR
- Foreign daily allowances follow the country table published each year.

If a meal is included in the trip (for example at a conference), the allowance is
reduced by half.

## Taxis and local transport

Use public transport when it is reasonable. Taxis are allowed when public
transport is not available, when you carry heavy equipment, or after 23:00.

## Expense reports

Submit expenses in the Expensify app within 30 days of the purchase. Attach a
photo of every receipt. Expenses older than 90 days are not reimbursed.

Company credit cards are available for employees who travel more than six times
a year. Apply through the finance team.
