# Polar Pixel Employee Handbook

Polar Pixel Oy is a fictional software company headquartered in Tampere, Finland.
This handbook describes how we work. It is sample content for the RAG examples.

## Working hours

Our standard working week is 37.5 hours. Core hours are 10:00–14:00 (Finnish time),
when everyone should be reachable. Outside core hours you plan your own day.

Overtime must be agreed with your manager in advance. Overtime hours can be taken
as paid time off or paid out at the end of each quarter.

## Remote work

You may work remotely up to three days per week. Teams agree on one shared office
day, which is usually Tuesday. Working from abroad is allowed for up to 20 working
days per calendar year, and only from EU or EEA countries.

Home office equipment is supported with a one-time budget of 600 EUR for a chair,
monitor, or desk. Ask the office manager for the order form.

## Vacation

Every employee earns 25 vacation days per year. After three full years of
employment you get 5 additional days, for a total of 30 days.

Summer vacation is held between June and August. At least two weeks of it must be
consecutive. Vacation requests for summer must be submitted by March 31.

Unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year, up to a maximum of
10 days.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, notify your manager by 09:00 on the first day. You can be away
for up to three days on your own notification. From the fourth day onwards a
doctor's certificate is required.

Occupational healthcare is provided by Terveystalo and covers general practitioner
visits, laboratory tests, and mental health support.

## Parental leave

Polar Pixel pays full salary for the first 8 weeks of parental leave, on top of
the statutory benefits from Kela. Let HR know at least two months before your
leave starts.

## Wellbeing benefits

Each employee gets a wellbeing benefit of 400 EUR per year on ePassi. It can be
used for sports, culture, and massage. Lunch is subsidised through the same card,
with a daily lunch benefit of 11.50 EUR.

The company sauna on the 6th floor is free to use on Fridays after 16:00.
