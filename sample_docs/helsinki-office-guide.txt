Helsinki office guide (fictional sample content, copied from an intranet web page)
Last updated by the office team. Questions? Ask in the #helsinki-office channel.

Our Helsinki office is on the 4th floor of Annankatu 12. The front door opens with your personal access badge between 06:00 and 22:00 on weekdays; on weekends the badge works from 08:00 to 18:00. New employees get their badge from the office manager on their first day, and a lost badge must be reported the same day so it can be deactivated. A replacement badge costs 20 EUR, deducted from the next salary unless the badge was stolen. Visitors are welcome, but every guest must be registered in the reception app before arrival and collected from the lobby by their host. Guests may not stay in the office without their host, and the host is responsible for them until they leave. The large meeting room Aurora seats 12 people and has a video conferencing system, while the two small rooms, Revontuli and Kaamos, seat four each. Book rooms in the calendar, keep bookings under three hours, and release a booking you no longer need so others can use the room. If a booked room is empty fifteen minutes after the start time, anyone may take it.

There is no car parking for employees at the office, but the building has a locked bicycle room in the courtyard, which your badge also opens. Showers and lockers are in the basement. If you come by car for a customer visit, park in the Kamppi public garage and claim the parking fee as a business expense.

The kitchen has free coffee, tea, fruit on Mondays and Thursdays, and oat milk in the fridge. Label your own food with your name and the date; the fridge is emptied every Friday at 15:00. Sort waste into bio, cardboard, plastic, metal, and mixed. The last person to leave turns off the coffee machine and checks that the balcony door is locked.
