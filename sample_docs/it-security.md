# IT Security Policy

Rules for keeping Polar Pixel Oy (fictional sample content) and our customers safe.

## Accounts and passwords

Use 1Password, the company password manager, for every work password. Passwords
must be at least 14 characters long and unique per service.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, GitHub, cloud consoles,
and the VPN. Use the Okta Verify app or a hardware security key. SMS codes are not
accepted.

## Devices

Company laptops have full-disk encryption enabled and are managed through our
device management system. Do not disable encryption, the firewall, or automatic
updates.

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. Share files through Google
Drive instead.

Install software only from the self-service portal. If you need something that
is not in the portal, open a ticket with IT.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to IT within one hour by calling the IT
hotline at +358 40 123 4567. IT will lock and wipe the device remotely.

## Phishing

Forward suspicious emails to security@polarpixel.example and then delete them.
Never enter your password on a page you reached from an email link. Reporting a
phishing attempt is always the right call, even if you already clicked.

## Data classification

All data belongs to one of four classes:

1. **Public** — may be shared freely, for example marketing material.
2. **Internal** — for employees only, for example this handbook.
3. **Confidential** — customer data and contracts; share only with people who need it.
4. **Secret** — encryption keys and credentials; stored only in 1Password or the
   cloud secret manager.

Confidential and Secret data must never be pasted into public AI tools.
