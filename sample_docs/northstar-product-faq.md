# Northstar Product FAQ

Northstar is Polar Pixel's analytics platform for e-commerce teams (fictional
sample content).

## What does Northstar do?

Northstar collects events from web shops and mobile apps, and turns them into
dashboards for conversion, retention, and revenue. It integrates with Shopify,
WooCommerce, and Magento out of the box.

## Pricing plans

| Plan       | Price per month | Users       | Event volume per month |
|------------|-----------------|-------------|------------------------|
| Starter    | 49 EUR          | up to 5     | 1 million              |
| Team       | 199 EUR         | up to 25    | 10 million             |
| Enterprise | custom          | unlimited   | custom                 |

Annual billing gives a 15% discount on Starter and Team plans.

## Service level

The Team plan has a 99.9% monthly uptime SLA. The Enterprise plan has a 99.95%
SLA and a named customer success manager. Starter has no SLA.

Support response times: Enterprise within 1 hour, Team within 8 business hours,
Starter within 2 business days.

## Data and privacy

All customer data is stored in the EU, in Google Cloud regions in Finland
(europe-north1) and Belgium (europe-west1). Raw event data is retained for
13 months, after which it is aggregated and the raw events are deleted.

Northstar is GDPR compliant and a data processing agreement (DPA) is included in
every plan.

## API

The REST API is available on all plans. The rate limit is 600 requests per minute
per workspace. API keys are created under Settings → Developers.

## Single sign-on

SAML single sign-on (SSO) with Okta, Azure AD, and Google Workspace is available
on the Enterprise plan only.

## Free trial

New workspaces get a 21-day free trial of the Team plan. No credit card is
required to start.
